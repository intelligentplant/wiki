App Store Connect operates on a distributed architecture.

App Store Connect software runs on an on-prem server...<br />
It connects to all varieties of process data historian...<br />
It may even be responsible for data collection and archiving from data sources such as Modbus, OPC, files-system,  Alarm & Event logs, etc...

In short, there are lots of places where a connection failure will impact data availability to end-users.

If you have a support contract with Intelligent Plant, we can help monitor your connectivity and make sure your data sources are always available.

Contact us for more information.
