The "Disabled Alarm Report" shows the top 20 disabled alarms along with KPIs:

- Count: How many times they have been disabled in the report period.
- Total Time (hours): The sum of durations of alarms disabled in the report period.

The "Disabled Alarm Report" can only be calculated if Alarm Analysis receives Operator Action events indicating when the Alarm is disabled and enabled.
