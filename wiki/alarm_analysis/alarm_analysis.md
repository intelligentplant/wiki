Alarm Analysis links to your control systems and provides an enterprise-wide set of KPIs and Analysis compliant with EEMUA 191 Rev 3. Several sets of bad actor types are identified and detailed.  Tag Analysis then allow individual bad actors to be quickly evaluated and their problems addressed.
