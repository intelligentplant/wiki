The Industrial App Store is a distributed architecture consisting of Apps, APIs, Data Connections and Data Sources.
Although we aim to limit disruption to App Store services, occasional maintenance is required.

Our general policy is to roll out updates on the final Friday of the month, though exceptions may be made for high priority items.

Subscribe to notifications for updates and/or refer to calendar below.

<strong>[Click here to sign up for scheduled maintenance email notifications.](https://intelligentplant.myfreshworks.com/crm/sales/web_forms/82c81e78b85c203f28acb774814c628abfd54f8443f808fbfec017ffa10b47d1/form.html)</strong>

### Maintenance Policy {: .title}

Below is our policy for managing uptime.

- <strong>Production</strong> <br /> Intelligent Plant <sup class="wiki-footnote" title="The Industrial App Store serves apps from multiple vendors. For non-Intelligent Plant apps, please refer to directly to the vendor for details of their update schedules.">[1]</sup> generally schedule updates and maintenance for <strong>Fridays 10:00-12:00 British Local Time</strong>. <br /> Exceptions may be made for high priority fixes. <br /> Refer to Maintenance Calendar for upcoming events.

- <strong>Pilot / Beta / Canary</strong> <br /> Pre-production apps and services may be updated at any time without notice.

- <strong>App Store Connects</strong> <br /> App Store Connect updates are distributed via NuGet package feeds. Although we prepare package feed updates for last Friday of every month, you can control <em>if</em> and <em>when</em> to collect them. For more detail, refer to [App Store Connect Updates](/data_core/app_store_connect_updates.html).
