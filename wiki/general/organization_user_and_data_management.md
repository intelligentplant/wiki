If you sign into the App Store using your work account, you can access the organization portal for your organization using the link under the My Account section of the App Store menu:

<img src="/wiki/assets/media/general/orgs/my-org-link.png" alt="Organization portal menu link" style="max-width:200px" loading="lazy" />

If you have been assigned to the [Administrators group](/wiki/general/organization_user_and_data_management/group_management.html) for your organization, you can perform various management actions, described in the following sections.

### Organization Portal {: .title}

The organization portal shows the current status of your organization:

![Organization portal (administrator view)](/wiki/assets/media/general/orgs/admin/admin-my-org.png)

At the bottom of the page, you will find a list of actions that you can perform. Note that the actions below are for administrator users only; regular users can only browse the other users from the organization who have joined the Industrial App Store, or browse the different groups that exist in the organization.

![Organization portal actions (administrator view)](/wiki/assets/media/general/orgs/admin/admin-my-org-actions.png)

The following links describe the different actions in more detail:

- [User Management](/wiki/general/organization_user_and_data_management/user_management.html)
- [Group Management](/wiki/general/organization_user_and_data_management/group_management.html)
- [App Security](/wiki/general/organization_user_and_data_management/app_access_management.html)
- [Trust Management](/wiki/general/organization_user_and_data_management/trust_management.html)
- [Audit Logs](/wiki/general/organization_user_and_data_management/audit_logs.html)
