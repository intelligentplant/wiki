Administrators can view audit log messages related to the organization by clicking on the <em>View the audit messages recorded for the organization</em> link in the organization portal action list.

<img src="/assets/media/general/organization_user_and_data_management/msedge_xwoe5bdg16.png" alt="msedge_xwoe5bdg16" style="max-width:700px" loading="lazy" />

Audit messages can be filtered by time range, priority, or content.
