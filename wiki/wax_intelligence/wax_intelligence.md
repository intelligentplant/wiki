As much as 85% of the worlds oil is likely to cause wax deposits.

Wax Intelligence:

- Scans your historian intelligently and identifies pipeline and infrastructure at risk of wax deposits

- Automatically generates a dashboard showing your current wax risk profile

- Can be used to alert you when you are in a waxing state
